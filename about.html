About

Luiza is a creative originally from Brazil and currently based in NYC. She graduated from Parsons School of Design with a major in Communication Design and a minor in Sociology, Management, and Technology. Her practice incorporates graphic design, branding, publications, art direction, digital marketing, and brand activations.

In past experiences, Luiza has worked with clients of varying scales. Regardless of the project, her approach focuses on communicating visual and strategic messages through an authentic, community driven, and humanized style.

In addition to her creative practice, Luiza maintains a strong connection to the sports world and nature, which deeply influences her professional interests and mindset. She is also obsessed with her dogs.
